Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Welcome to Spark. Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Spark ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use the Spark mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Profile details you provide such as name, age, gender, dating intent, bio, and profile photos.
  • Location Information: With your permission, precise or approximate geographical location to calculate proximity and display nearby prospective matches.
  • Media & Verification: Photos and optional verification selfies captured via your camera to verify profile authenticity.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Matches, likes, swipes, and messaging history necessary to facilitate connections.
  • Optional Connected Accounts: Spotify listening preferences, top music genres, and anthems if connected.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your user profile and match recommendations.
  • To calculate compatibility scores, celestial zodiac synergy, and mutual music tastes.
  • To power safety features including fake call triggers, user reporting, and harassment prevention.
  • To process subscription payments securely via Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit).

3. How We Protect Your Data

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS in transit and AES-256 at rest). We do not sell your personal identifying data to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

4. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You may edit or update your profile at any time in the app settings. You have the right to request full deletion of your account and associated data by navigating to Settings ➔ Delete Account or contacting our support team directly.
