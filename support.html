👑 Managing Subscriptions & Free Trials

Spark VIP subscriptions (Gold, Platinum, Black Edition) include a 3-day free trial and are billed through Apple Media Services.

To cancel or change your plan at any time:

1. Open your iPhone Settings ➔ Tap your Apple ID name at the top.

2. Tap Subscriptions ➔ Select Spark.

3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

🛡️ Safety & Reporting

Your safety is our highest priority. To report an inappropriate profile or fraudulent user:

Tap the three dots (...) on the user's card or chat screen and select Report / Block User. Our moderation team reviews reports 24/7.
